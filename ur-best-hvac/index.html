Boiler
questions

The questions that come up on nearly every boiler call, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

How is a boiler different from a water heater?

A boiler delivers hot water through a closed-loop system that runs steam through radiators for space heating, which returns to the boiler as hot water. A hot water tank just heats and delivers water through hot water faucets — as water, not steam.

Where is the line between the two, exactly?

More specifically, a boiler is a water heating device that exceeds all of the following: 120 gallons water storage, 160 psi operating pressure, 210 degrees Fahrenheit temperature, and 199,000 Btu/hr heat input.

Can any HVAC company repair or replace a boiler?

No. Not every HVAC company is licensed by the state to repair and replace boilers. Only a few licensed boiler contractors will work on residential boilers. A boiler licence is required by the state to pull permits, repair large sections of line, and install new boilers. Ours is Kentucky Boiler Licence KY2499.
