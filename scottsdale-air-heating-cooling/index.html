How often should I have my air conditioner serviced in Phoenix?

At least once a year, ideally in spring before triple-digit temperatures arrive. Valley AC systems run nearly nonstop from May through September, so an annual tune-up is the single best way to avoid a mid-summer breakdown.

Why is my air conditioner running but not cooling the house?

The most common culprits are a clogged air filter, a tripped breaker, low refrigerant from a leak, or a frozen evaporator coil.

How long does an air conditioner last in Arizona?

Expect 10–15 years from a well-maintained system here — a bit less than in milder climates, simply because the desert works equipment so much harder.

How often should I change my AC air filter?

During Phoenix summers, check standard 1-inch filters every 30 days — our dust, monsoon storms, and near-constant runtime clog filters faster than the usual 90-day guidance assumes.

Why does my air conditioner keep turning on and off?

Short cycling is usually caused by an oversized system, a dirty filter, a refrigerant problem, or a thermostat mounted in direct sunlight — a common issue in Valley homes.

How often should my water heater be flushed?

At least once a year — and in the Phoenix area that’s not optional advice. We have some of the hardest water in the country, and sediment builds fast.

Should I use store-bought chemical drain cleaner?

We don’t recommend it. Chemical cleaners corrode pipes over time, usually clear only part of the clog, and are hazardous to handle.

Why do my drains keep clogging?

Recurring clogs signal a deeper issue: hardened grease and debris coating the pipe walls, a venting problem, or tree roots growing into the sewer line — common in established Phoenix and Scottsdale neighborhoods.
