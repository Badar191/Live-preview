What we do

Residential heating and air conditioning, plus light commercial ductless. All makes, models and brands.

  • Service & repair

    Furnace and air conditioning service and repair on all makes, models and brands. Diagnostic service calls are $129, and you get a detailed proposal and cost of repair estimate before anything happens.

  • Installation & replacement

    Central heating and air conditioning equipment, installed or replaced. Free estimates on new and replacement systems, and an on-site quote rather than a number posted to you a week later.

  • Ductless mini splits

    Ductless mini split heating and air conditioners, residential and light commercial. Attic rooms, additions, sun porches, garages — the places a duct run was never going to reach.

  • Ductwork

    Ductwork repair and installation. Replacing collapsed flex, sealing what leaks, and running new trunk-and-branch where the old work has given up.

  • Seasonal maintenance

    Spring and fall tune-ups, one system at a time. Available as a one-off or on an annual agreement — both prices are further down this page.

  • Indoor air quality

    Improving indoor air quality in your East Nashville home, including whole-house media filtration and in-duct ionisation. The consultation is free.

  • Attic insulation

    The best possible heating, cooling, ductwork and insulation products — because a right-sized system in an under-insulated attic is still going to run all afternoon.

What does it cost to have someone come out?

$129 for the service trip and the diagnostic. You get a detailed proposal and cost of repair estimate before any repair work starts, so the price is agreed before the wrench comes out.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free for new and replacement heating and cooling systems, and the indoor air quality consultation is free as well. Quotes and invoices are done on site.

What warranty comes with an installation?

A 5-year warranty on labor, and we take care of registering your product for the 10-year parts warranty so you are not the one chasing the paperwork.

Will you push me into a maintenance contract?

No. We won’t force you into unnecessary and costly service agreements or seasonal maintenance contracts. A single tune-up is $220 for heating or cooling. If you do want the Preventative Maintenance Agreement it is $336 per system annually, and it is required to uphold labor warranties on equipment we install after the first year.

Can I finance a new system?

We offer financing through Synchrony Bank. Pending qualification and credit approval, you may be eligible for up to 18 months of financing at 0% interest.

Do you work on my brand of equipment?

Service and repair covers all makes, models and brands. The equipment we most often install and work with is Rheem, Mitsubishi, Honeywell and Nu-Calgon’s iWave-R.

Do you come out to my part of town?

East Nashville and Madison, TN. That is the whole service area, on purpose — staying close is what lets us turn up promptly and stay present through a long job.

What if I’m not happy with the work?

100% satisfaction guaranteed or your money back. We guarantee our work and we’ll never charge you for that guarantee.
