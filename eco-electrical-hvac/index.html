Helpful tips

Questions, answered.

How often should heating and cooling equipment be looked at?

Have a professional inspect your ducts and cooling equipment to keep the system performing, and clean or replace air conditioning filters every month. Keeping vents, baseboard heaters and ducts free of dust does much of the rest.

What can I do myself to cut energy use?

Seal air leaks in the attic, basement, crawlspaces and around windows and doors, check that the attic has adequate insulation, and draw shades on sunny sides of the house during peak hours. Clean rain gutters and storm drains to prevent water damage.

How do I keep indoor air healthy?

Replace the batteries in smoke, radon and carbon monoxide detectors when the clocks change. Consider a ventilation system to improve air circulation, clean or replace water-damaged carpets and materials to prevent mould, and use fuel-burning appliances only with proper ventilation.

Do you handle fire alarm systems?

Yes — technicians are trained on Siemens, Potter, Microm, Simplex, Notifier and Edwards systems, covering new installations, system upgrades and replacement of an existing setup.

Do you work on commercial and industrial sites?

Yes. Eco Electrical HVAC runs residential, commercial and industrial work — from home renovations to commercial retrofits, plant power and backup generator installations.
