About us

One goal, held
since 2008

Established in 2008, Modern Mechanical is a customer-focused company in the Hudson County, New Jersey area. Our highly trained professionals have one goal: to make our company the best plumbing, heating and cooling company in Northeastern New Jersey — by providing unparalleled service, top-quality products and expert knowledge in the industry.

We are experts in heating and cooling, plumbing, air quality and more. Alongside the service work, we offer peace of mind: making sure your equipment runs safely, helping you save on fuel bills, and delivering relief to anyone concerned about the quality of the air they breathe.
