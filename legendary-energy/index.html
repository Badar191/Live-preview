Legendary Energy story

From our family
to yours.

Legendary Energy is a family-owned HVAC business serving the greater Phoenix area.

Our owner and founder, Victor Ramirez, created Legendary Energy after more than 7 years in the industry. He loved working with his hands and quickly grew his knowledge in insulation and HVAC.

While Victor loved his work and the industry, he didn’t like the over promise and underdeliver strategy. He knew that unnecessary upsells and high-pressure sales strategies weren’t in customers’ best interests. That’s why Victor founded Legendary Energy.

Our business model is built on transparency and trust — because we know good karma is good business. Today, our mission is to offer high-quality service at affordable prices. Excellence is even in our name: we want every customer to have a “legendary” experience with our team that other HVAC providers just can’t match.
