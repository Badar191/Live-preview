About

Family owned, working in Tampa Bay since 1993

Smart Heating & Cooling is a family-owned and locally operated HVAC company based in St. Petersburg, FL, with its office in Pinellas Park. Our team serves homeowners and businesses across Pinellas, Hillsborough, Pasco and Manatee counties — honest, dependable air conditioning and heating services you can count on.

We specialise in AC repair, installation and maintenance for all major brands, combining experience, integrity and precision to keep Florida homes comfortable year-round. As a family-owned company we bring the personalised care of a small business to jobs of any size.

  • Honesty & transparencyClear communication and upfront pricing every time.
  • Reliable serviceOn-time appointments, clean workmanship, and lasting results.
  • Community focusProudly serving Tampa Bay families and supporting local growth.
  • Long-term careHelping you extend system life and lower energy costs.

Written estimates with no surprises or hidden fees · Licensed & insured · All major brands serviced · Financing options available
