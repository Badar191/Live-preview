What we do

Three systems, one set of hands.

Heating, cooling and the air moving between them are one machine, not three. Treating them separately is how a house ends up with a brand-new furnace and the same cold back bedroom.

Heating

Furnaces and heat pumps — service, repair and replacement. Diagnosis first, so you know whether you're buying a part or a system.

Cooling

Central air and ductless systems. Most “the AC is broken” calls turn out to be airflow, charge or a thermostat — we check those before quoting a unit.

Ventilation & airflow

Hot rooms, cold rooms, weak vents, stale air. The unglamorous half of the system, and usually the half that's actually wrong.

How it works

No mystery between the call and the price.

Four steps, in this order, every time. If a step is going to change what you pay, you hear about it at that step — not on the invoice.

  • You call, we listen properly What the system is doing, how long it's been doing it, and what's already been tried. Most of the diagnosis starts here.
  • We come and look At the equipment and at the ductwork feeding it. A price quoted down a phone line is a guess with a number attached.
  • You get the options, in plain words Repair it, service it, or replace it — with what each one costs and what it buys you. The choice stays yours.
  • The work gets done, and tidied up Agreed scope, agreed price, and the house left the way we found it.

Before you call

Two minutes that save an hour

  • Find the sticker Make, model and serial are printed on the furnace or on the outdoor unit. Photograph it — it tells us the age and the parts before we arrive.
  • Note what it's doing Short-cycling, blowing cold, a rattle at start-up, a smell. Symptoms narrow it faster than a model number does.
  • Check the easy two A filter that hasn't been changed and a thermostat on the wrong mode account for more callouts than any component.

Common questions

Straight answers.

Which areas do you cover?

Scarborough, East York and the neighbouring east end of Toronto. If you're just outside that, call and ask rather than assuming — the answer depends on the day more than the map.

Do you handle both heating and cooling?

Yes. In most homes they share the same blower and the same ductwork, so looking at one without the other only tells you half the story.

What should I have ready when I call?

The make, model and serial from the sticker on the unit, roughly how old it is, and a plain description of what it's doing. That's usually enough to know what to bring.
