FAQs, answered

Before you invest in a system.

Can I get my old HVAC system repaired?

Often, yes — minor faults are straightforward for an experienced technician. The rule of thumb we use: if the repair would cost about 30% of a new system, or your utility bills have gone up by half, replacing it is the better money.

Would I save money by replacing my old system?

New systems run up to 60% more efficient than older home comfort systems, so yes — on the energy bill. Sometimes a thorough inspection finds that only a few components need replacing, and we'll tell you that instead.

What should I consider before buying a new system?

Start with a reliable contractor near you who is associated with a recognised brand — the right one will help you choose rather than sell you the largest unit. A central system gives better indoor air quality and humidity control overall, and a programmable thermostat is worth having for accurate, reliable performance.

What is an air handler?

It circulates conditioned air through the house. Adding one — often alongside an air purifier — keeps the air moving properly and clear of pollutants.

Do you really answer at 3am?

We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for emergencies. A furnace failing in January is not a next-business-day problem.
