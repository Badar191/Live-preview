Common questions

Good to know before you call

Do you charge for an estimate?

Estimates on new equipment are free. For a diagnostic visit on an existing system there's a service call fee, which we'll tell you up front when you book — never after the fact.

What if my furnace fails overnight or on a weekend?

Our answering service runs 24/7. Call 416-598-4115 and we'll get a message to the on-call technician. Heat-out calls in winter get priority.

Which brands do you install?

We hold the Certified Comfort Expert designation for York furnaces and air conditioners, and we're a Fujitsu Elite dealer with one of the highest ductless installation rates in Toronto. We also service most other makes and models.

Can you work on my boiler as well as my furnace?

Yes. We handle mid- and high-efficiency boilers, radiant floor heating and hydronic systems alongside forced-air furnaces — it's all in-house.

Do you do commercial work?

We do. We've served Toronto businesses as well as families for over 55 years, from small storefronts up to multi-unit buildings.

How often should I have my system serviced?

Once a year for heating and once for cooling is the standard we recommend. It keeps warranties valid, catches small faults before they become emergencies, and keeps running costs down.
