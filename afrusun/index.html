Common questions

Before the site visit.

Do you do commercial as well as houses?

Both, and the commercial side is the specialism: new equipment installation, system upgrades and retrofitting from rooftop units to VRF for commercial and condominium buildings.

Can you help with rebates and financing?

Yes. Afrusun runs Rebate, Financing and Rental programmes alongside the installation work, plus rebates and incentives for energy-saving HVAC.

Will you design a system, or only install one?

Both. HVAC consultation and custom HVAC design are offered in their own right, along with inspections, system optimisation and warranty services.

Which brands do you install?

Carrier, Lennox, Bosch, Goodman, Amana, York, Fujitsu, Rinnai, Aprilaire, GeneralAire and KoolKing.

Do you handle geothermal and floor heating?

Yes — geothermal HVAC systems and floor heating both sit within the heating range, alongside furnaces, heat pumps, boilers and fireplaces.
