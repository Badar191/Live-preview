Before you call

Repair, or replace?

Is it time to replace my system?

If your HVAC system is more than ten years old and starting to lose efficiency, it's worth asking. Age and inefficiency are the common signs — but they aren't the whole answer, and we'd rather walk you through the call than make it for you.

Do you come out at night and on weekends?

Yes — 24/7 emergency HVAC service. A furnace failing in the middle of a Toronto January is not a next-business-day problem.

Can you get me out of a water heater rental contract?

Yes. Contract removal and replacement is a service we offer, and the unit we install is covered by a ten-year parts and one-year labour warranty.

What's a red tag, and can you clear it?

A red tag is issued when a gas appliance is found unsafe and has to be shut off. We do emergency gas repairs including red tag removal — that's what the gas fitting licence is for.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Detailed preventative maintenance packages are available, and they're customised per home rather than sold as one fixed tier.
