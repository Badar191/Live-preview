Questions
people ask

Short answers to what people ask before they book. If yours is not here, call and ask — someone picks up.

Call 503-636-5371
Do you answer after hours?

“We offer 24 hour emergency service because problems can happen at any time.” Comfort Club members get “after hours emergency service billed at standard daytime rates with no overtime charges.”

Are the technicians certified?

“All our HVAC Service Technicians are NATE Certified” and “We require our technicians to be NATE Certified.” On top of that: “All of our employees are Trust Certified including Background checks and drug testing.”

Are you licensed and insured?

“Licensed, Bonded & Insured CCB #161325.” The same number is printed on our logo and on the side of the van.

What equipment do you install?

We are an American Standard Customer Care Dealer: “this means we have met rigorous standards based partly on customer feedback.” Our current offers cover the American Standard AccuClean whole house cleaner and HoneyWell or American Standard programmable comfort controls, and we show the Nexia thermostat app.

What does the Comfort Club include?

One or two precision tune-up and cleanings each year, parts, materials and labor discounted 15%, after-hours emergency service at standard daytime rates, a free extended repair warranty, free priority service, and Filter Club included at no charge. The tune-up is their 21-point precision clean: once a year for a furnace, twice for furnace and air conditioning. And: “Try the Comfort Club Program for ninety days and if you are not completely satisfied we will refund your money.”

Can you help with rebates or financing?

“We put together qualifying systems with the highest payback available. We even fill out the application forms and send to the appropriate agencies for you.” Financing is offered through Wells Fargo and GreenSky, and we are a Trade Ally of Energy Trust of Oregon.

Which towns do you cover?

The entire Portland metro area: Portland, Clackamas, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tualatin, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Oregon City, Lake Grove, Gresham, West Linn, Gladstone, King City, Oak Grove, Milwaukie, Sunnyside, Progress and Wilsonville.

Do you do anything besides heating and cooling?

Yes. Gas lines, gas fireplaces, water heaters, venting and chimney liners for furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers, electronic air cleaners, energy recovery ventilators, dehumidifiers and humidifiers, duct work — and a full-service sheet metal shop behind all of it.

Is there anything in it for referring a friend?

“If you were referred to us by one of our customers, You will get $50.00 off the installation of a heat pump, gas furnace or any new equipment, and they will get $50.00 to say ‘Thank You.’”
