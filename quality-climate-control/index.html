Common questions

Asked on the phone, answered here.

What happens when I call?

You talk to Josh — not a booking service. Describe what the AC is doing, and you'll either get a time slot or, if it sounds like something simple, a thing to try first. Nobody bills you for a phone call.

Do you work on my brand of air conditioner?

Almost certainly. Residential central air and ductless systems share the same fundamentals across brands. If yours is something genuinely unusual, you'll hear that on the phone — not after a service visit.

When should I book a tune-up?

Spring, before the first hot stretch. A serviced system starts the season clean, and anything wearing out gets caught while it's still a small part instead of a July emergency.

Repair or replace?

Whichever the numbers actually support. Age, the fault, and what the fix costs against what a new unit costs — you'll get that math straight, and the decision stays yours.

Do you handle heating too?

Cooling is the day-to-day specialty. If it's a heating question, call anyway — worst case you'll get pointed in the right direction instead of left on hold.
