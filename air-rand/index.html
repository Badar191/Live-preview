Questions

Before you call us out.

When are you actually open?

Monday to Sunday, 24 hours. Seven days a week is the whole point of the name — a system failing on a Saturday night is still a failure.

Residential or commercial?

Both. The same care and expertise is applied to the smallest residential project and the most complex commercial installation.

Do you handle gas lines and water as well as HVAC?

Yes. Heating, cooling, ventilation, ductwork, gas lines, water systems and indoor air quality are all in scope — which means one contractor rather than four.

Is the work warrantied?

Every system installed is supported by comprehensive warranties, and maintenance, repairs and performance checks are available afterwards.

How quickly will I hear back?

Promptly — clear communication and quick turnaround are the two things AIRRAND commits to publicly, so hold us to them.
