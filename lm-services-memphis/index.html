Questions

Questions L&M gets asked.

Answered only from what L&M already publishes. Anything not here is a question for the phone.

How far out will you come?

L&M generally makes HVAC service and installation calls within a 100 mile radius of Memphis, TN — Memphis and East Memphis, Germantown, Collierville, Bartlett, Cordova, Arlington, Lakeland, Millington, Covington and Oakland in Tennessee; Horn Lake, Nesbit, Senatobia, Oxford and North Mississippi; and Marion, Arkansas. If you are not sure, call and ask.

Residential, commercial, or both?

Both. Air conditioning service, heating and furnace service, installation and inspection are all offered residential and commercial, on HVAC systems, heat pumps, air conditioners, furnaces and boilers.

What does the $75 tune-up cover?

The summer version is an HVAC tune-up, cleaning and safety check. The winter version is a furnace inspection and safety check. Both are priced at $75 on L&M's own promotions.

Do you work on my brand?

L&M installs and services equipment from all major, reliable and energy-efficient brands — Carrier, Goodman, Lennox, Ruud, Tempstar and Trane are the marks shown on their own brand line. No matter the brand, L&M will help you live comfortably.

Repair it, or replace it?

Repairing heating and air equipment can add years to a system's operation. In some situations requiring major repairs, replacement makes more sense. If you aren't certain which applies, an inspection is the way to settle it — and equipment financing is available if replacement wins.

When are you open, and what about emergencies?

Monday to Friday, 7:00am to 5:00pm, with Saturday by appointment. Emergency service is available — for an emergency, call 901.487.8921 rather than using the form.

Not covered here?

L&M is on the phone Monday to Friday, 7:00am to 5:00pm, and Saturday by appointment. Emergency service is available outside that.
