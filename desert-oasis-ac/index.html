About the family

Your comfort,
our commitment.

Desert Oasis AC Services is family‑owned and locally operated out of Chandler. We started this because the desert doesn’t forgive a dead air conditioner — blistering summers, cold snaps in winter, and a system that has to keep up with both.

So we keep it simple. Our technicians show up on time, tell you straight what’s wrong, and fix it so it holds — whether that’s repairing an air conditioner, installing a new A/C system, replacing fan motors or sorting out ductwork. We work in English and Spanish, because being understood is half the job. Se habla español.

Desert Oasis AC Services is fully licensed and bonded with the Arizona Registrar of Contractors, ROC #347635. That’s the one badge we’ll show you — it means the work is done to standard and stands behind itself.
