The specialty

Our specialty is hydronics — both steam and hot water.

Where we excel is in the proper evaluation and repair of boiler systems, and our understanding of older vintage systems and equipment.

  • Steam boilers — headers, pressuretrols, gauge glass
  • Hot water boilers, circulators and expansion tanks
  • Radiator valves and vents
  • Near-boiler piping rebuilt in copper or black iron
  • Indirect tanks and commercial water heaters

Since 1949

One address, one trade,
three quarters of a century.

We were founded in 1949 in the East English Village neighborhood on the east side of Detroit, where we remain to this day. When times got tough and businesses left the area throughout the years, we continued to invest in our city.

To be in business over 75 years we had to earn our community’s trust with excellent customer service, a knowledgeable staff, and honest and fair pricing. We are family owned, and we serve the entire Metro Detroit Tri-County area.

Answers

Straight answers

If yours is not here, call the shop on 313.885.2400 and ask for it plainly.

Do you work on steam and hot water boilers?

Yes. Hydronics is our specialty, both steam and hot water. Where we excel is in the proper evaluation and repair of boiler systems, and our understanding of older vintage systems and equipment.

Can I buy parts without booking a service call?

Yes. The parts counter is open to the public and we stock parts for most name brands — filters and humidifier pads, control boards, gas valves, blower and draft inducer motors, ignitors and flame sensors, pressure and limit switches, radiator valves and vents, condensate pumps and more. We cut gauge glass at the counter too.

What are your showroom hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

Do you handle commercial rooftop units?

We have the knowledge and resources to properly and safely replace a rooftop unit — from managing the use of a crane to installing the new unit and disposing of the old one. Commercial heating, cooling, ventilation, refrigeration and hot line equipment are all part of the work.

Which areas do you cover?

Detroit, including our own East English Village neighborhood, and the entire Metro Detroit Tri-County area.

Do you install ductless mini-splits and high-velocity systems?

Yes. We are a proud Mitsubishi Diamond Dealer, from single heads in just one room to whole home installation, and a preferred Unico installer for high-velocity air conditioning.

Is financing available?

We work with the Wells Fargo Home Projects card, Synchrony Bank and Michigan Saves. Ask us for the terms that are current when you order.

How is pricing handled?

You will know the full cost of the project before committing. There are no hidden fees and no tricky buy-one-get-one deals that just hide the math — you get honesty and integrity with upfront pricing.

Are you hiring?

We are accepting applications for installers, service technicians and boiler installers, each with a minimum of three years’ experience. We offer a very competitive wage and benefit package including health insurance, vision, dental and 401k.
