Common questions

Before the site visit.

Do you do refrigeration as well as HVAC?

Both — it's in the company description. Heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration, which for a restaurant or a food retailer means one contractor instead of two.

How long has the company been going?

An HVAC contractor since 1985.

Are you licensed?

Licensed, TSSA and insured — their own wording, and the paperwork a commercial gas job requires.

Can you replace a rooftop unit without shutting us down?

A rooftop swap is the routine job here: the old unit comes off, the new one goes on the curb, and the gas and electrical are reconnected. Ask for the timeline when you get the estimate.

Is there financing?

Yes — financing is available, alongside maintenance and protection plans and preventive maintenance agreements.

Do you work on houses too?

Yes. Gas furnaces, boilers, tankless hot water, air conditioning and ductless heat pumps — including condo fan coils and condo heat pumps.
