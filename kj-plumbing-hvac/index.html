NINJAtips

A few things worth knowing before you pick up the phone.

Low water pressure at a faucet

If your water pressure is low, then check the aerator in your faucets.

A water heater that pops and bangs

If water heater is making a popping sound, then the water heater may need flushed.

A tankless heater that has lost its heat

If a tankless water heater isn't heating the water like it did when it was new, then it may need to be descaled because of poor water quality.

A sewer smell from a drain you never use

If you smell a sewer smell and you have a drain that doesn't get used very much (like a guest bathroom, or floor drain) — pour water down that drain, it may have dried out from not being used.

Keeping an eye on the deep freezer

Put a night light behind deep freezer using the same outlet, so you know if your power is being supplied to the freezer without opening it.

Drain maintenance without calling anyone

Bio-clean is a great drain maintenance without a plumber, it is sold on Amazon.
