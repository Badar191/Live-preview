The firm

A true partner, not a subcontractor

We have delivered thousands of successful projects for healthcare, institutional, industrial and commercial clients. Our primary objective is to protect your project deadlines and budgets. You can rely on us to provide the best advice the industry has to offer.

Our mission

Drives us to provide sound engineering design and consulting to solve the complex challenges of our Business Owner, Architect, and Contractor partners. In doing so, we are proud to play an integral role in positively impacting people and communities through the functionality of space, the efficiency of operation, and the safety of occupants and those whom they serve.

Our vision

Is to build long-term trusted relationships with our clients and industry partners through consistent, authentic communication and personal service. We do this by investing in our staff with our JDR Academy and Continuing Education Program — technical training and application, and critical communication and leadership skills. Everyone at JDR is a leader: of themselves, of others, and of the organization.

Questions

Short answers

Every answer below is our own published wording. If something is not here, call and ask.

608.277.1728
What do you design in-house?

Nine service groups: mechanical and HVAC systems; plumbing and fire protection; electrical; energy engineering; sustainable design; commissioning; technology; building information modeling; and other design and drafting. Every one of them is listed in full on this page under Disciplines.

Where are you licensed to work?

JDR Engineering is currently licensed to perform work in 17 states around the country. We provide full MEP design services for these states: Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, North Dakota, Ohio, South Dakota, Texas, Virginia and Wisconsin.

Who is actually running my project?

We structure our project teams to include one principal on every project, involved from start to finish. Our principals are extremely knowledgeable in market trends and strategic, big-picture thinking, yet are passionate about building trust, sharing innovative design practices, and providing the best customer experience possible.

What credentials does your staff hold?

JDR Engineering, Inc. is licensed as a professional design firm and has licensed Professional Engineers, LEED accredited engineers, and designers with their Designer of Engineering Systems License. Individual staff also hold Lighting Certified (NCQLP) and IESNA credentials, E.I.T. registration, and the ASSE 6060 Medical Gas Systems Designer Certification.

Do you work on LEED projects?

Yes. Whether our clients wish to pursue LEED Certification or not, we are committed to designing projects that are better for our environment and better for our communities. Our certified register runs from Goodwill SE Wisconsin at Certified through the Union Corners medical office building at Silver to RenewAire, Edgewood College's The Stream and the Madison Fire Station buildings at Gold.

What do you model and draft in?

Revit, AutoCAD and Navisworks. Under Building Information Modeling and Other Design or Drafting we also list BIM coordination, floor plan drafting and historical design.

How much work do you take on in a year?

Approximately 300 projects completed annually, across commercial and cultural, laboratories, industrial, healthcare, government, educational and institutional, and data centre markets.

Are you hiring?

We currently list one opening, BIM Specialist. Benefits include Group Medical Insurance, Dental Insurance, Life/Accidental Death Insurance, Short Term Disability, Long Term Disability, a 401(k) Salary Deferral Plan, Tuition Reimbursement and Professional Societies.
