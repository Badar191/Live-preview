Common questions

Before you book the visit.

Do you cover emergencies?

Yes — emergency service 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call for fast, dependable service rather than waiting on a form.

Residential or commercial?

Both. Naz works across custom homes, existing houses and commercial buildings, including rooftop unit installation and commercial heating and cooling.

Are your technicians licensed?

Experienced, bonded, trained and licensed technical specialists install and service the equipment. Naz is also a registered TSSA contractor, audited regularly for quality assurance.

Do you do the sheet metal as well as the unit?

Yes. Sheet metal construction, vents and ducts, gas lines and furnace placement are all part of the custom home work — which is why a new build doesn't need three separate trades.

What areas do you serve?

Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area, from the Scarborough base.
