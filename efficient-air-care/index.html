Frequently asked

Straight answers, no upsell.

How often should I check my air filter?

About every 30 days. For best results, arrange regular check-ups so maintenance stops being something you have to remember.

Why does regular maintenance matter?

It keeps the system running smoothly and prevents costly repairs, which saves money over the life of the equipment rather than on the day.

Are all HVAC filters the same?

No. Residential filters are generally smaller than those used in commercial buildings like businesses and hospitals, and the choice has a real impact on your air quality. We'll help you find the right one for your space.

Can indoor air be hazardous?

It can, if it carries pollutants like carbon monoxide or mould. We have the tools, knowledge and skill to identify potential contaminants, and can install an air quality monitor so you can check for yourself.

How do I get rid of indoor odours?

Working ventilation is essential. Our air vent cleaning service is usually where to start — masking the smell doesn't fix the airflow causing it.

How do I choose the right humidifier?

It depends on your needs. There are cool mist, warm mist, ultrasonic and evaporative types as well as vaporizers — worth a short conversation rather than a guess.

What does HVAC actually stand for?

Heating, ventilation and air conditioning — the central system that heats and cools a building, rather than any one piece of equipment in it.
