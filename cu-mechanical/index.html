Common questions

Asked before the first visit.

What does HVAC stand for?

Heating, ventilation and air conditioning. It's the shorthand for your complete heating and cooling system rather than any one piece of it.

Why is my HVAC making noise?

Some noise is normal as a system runs, and how much depends on details like the age of the equipment. A change in the noise is the part worth calling about — that's usually the system telling you something early.

What regular maintenance should I expect?

Your system is the main way air moves through the building, so the priority is keeping that airflow unrestricted and clean. Indoor and outdoor units both need regular attention: filters actually removing dust and debris, and heat exchangers and coils kept clear.

Do you cover emergencies?

Office hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 5pm. Emergencies are covered 24/7 — a heating system failing in a Toronto January is not a next-business-day problem.
