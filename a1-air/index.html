The A1 story

A tank mechanic, a navy man, and a third generation.

Almost every HVAC company says "family owned". This one published the dates — so here they are.

1940s–50s

Walter Messenger first learns refrigeration during the Second World War, as a Royal Engineer tank mechanic in the British army. After the war he is hired as a London bus mechanic.

1960–1967

Walter and the Messenger family emigrate to Canada. Walter decides to open his own business. A1 Air Conditioning & Heating is born.

1968–1974

Walter's son Paul joins the Canadian navy as an aircraft mechanic. After his service he comes home and enrols in the very first refrigeration class held at Mohawk College in Hamilton.

1980–1982

A1 moves its head office into the original Township of Trafalgar building on Chartwell Road in southeast Oakville. On Walter's retirement, Paul takes over the family business.

1984

A1 creates the SuperCare Protection Plan — the first programme of its kind in the industry, giving customers an annual maintenance plan with yearly tune-ups included.

1989

The product line expands into indoor air quality — purifiers, ventilators, humidifiers. Paul's son Peter sets out to continue the business, qualifying as a gas fitter, electrician and refrigeration mechanic.

1990s

A1 offers a ten-year warranty including parts and labour on every installation — the first of its kind in southern Ontario.

2005

On Paul's retirement, Peter Messenger takes over the family business. Third generation.

2011

On the hottest day of the year, fire breaks out at the Chartwell Road office. Every employee gets out safely, Oakville firefighters put it out, and A1 finds temporary space nearby.

Today

After years of carrying a wide range of brands, A1 becomes the first Daikin dealer in Canada — and holds ISO 45001 certification for occupational health and safety.

Common questions

Asked before the first visit.

Do I get an actual person when I call?

Yes. A1's stated promise is that a real person answers the phone, and the line runs 24 hours a day, every day of the year. The emergency number is (647) 493-0576.

Are the technicians employees or subcontractors?

Employees — long-time ones. A1 states that it never uses contractors, and that its technicians are triple-certified.

What is a protection plan?

An annual maintenance programme with yearly tune-ups and other services included. A1 created the SuperCare plan in 1984, the first programme of its kind in the industry.

Which brands do you install?

A1 carried a wide range of brands for years and became the first Daikin dealer in Canada — Daikin, founded in Japan in 1924, is the largest heating and air conditioning manufacturer in the world.

Do you do electrical work too?

Yes — home electrical, including panel upgrades, alongside the heating, cooling, water heating and air quality work.
