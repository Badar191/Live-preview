Common questions

Before you call.

Do you really answer at 2am?

Yes. We provide 24-hour emergency HVAC service across the Greater Toronto Area. Call 416-532-2859 and we'll get a technician moving — no-heat calls in winter are prioritized.

Are estimates free?

Estimates on new equipment are free with no obligation. For diagnostics on an existing system we'll quote the service call fee up front when you book.

Which brands are you authorized on?

We're authorized dealers for Carrier and Mitsubishi, and we regularly work with Viessmann, Rinnai and Weil-McLain equipment. We repair and install most other makes and models as well.

Do you handle commercial and industrial buildings?

We do — residential, commercial and industrial. We've been working across all three since 1964 and offer peace-of-mind service contracts for properties that can't afford downtime.

My furnace is old. Repair or replace?

It depends on age, efficiency and what's failed. We'll give you an honest read on both paths with pricing for each, so you can decide — we're not paid to push a replacement.

What areas do you cover?

Toronto and the surrounding GTA, including North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, Mississauga and Oakville. If you're unsure, give us a call.
