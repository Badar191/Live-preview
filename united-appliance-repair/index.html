Who we are

A small Bay Area shop that grew up answering the phone.

United Appliance Repair started in 2015 as a small company set on fast, reliable, affordable repairs for our community. We've grown enough to offer same-day service across most Bay Area counties — and stayed close enough that the way we treat your home still matters more than anything.

Our prices are flat-rate and upfront, so you don't have to worry about the number climbing while the job goes on. From the first call to the moment we leave, the goal is the simplest, least-stressful repair you've had.

01 — Respect

Treat people the way we'd want to be treated: listen to understand, respond in good time, and speak plainly and without sarcasm.

02 — Integrity

Make only the agreements we intend to keep, and flag a problem the first moment we see one. Say nothing about anyone we wouldn't say to their face.

03 — Customer focus

Understand what the job in front of us actually needs, do it right, and have a bit of fun in the process.

Before you book

Yes. Alongside home appliances we handle commercial refrigerators, freezers, ice machines, ovens, washers and dryers for restaurants and businesses.
