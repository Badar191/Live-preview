Common questions

Before the first call.

Do you fabricate your own sheet metal?

Yes — sheet metal is one of the four trades the company is certified and insured for, alongside heating, air conditioning and refrigeration. Ductwork and air distribution systems are made as well as fitted.

What kinds of buildings do you work in?

Commercial and institutional: schools, hospitals, fire stations, college campuses and commercial buildings.

Can you move an existing walk-in cooler?

Yes. Dismantle and relocate is a service in its own right, alongside installation and service and repair.

Is the consultation really free?
