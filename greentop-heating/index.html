What size HVAC system should I have?

The proper size depends on a Manual J load calculation that factors in square footage, insulation levels, window type, orientation, and local climate. An oversized or undersized unit can lead to higher energy bills and poor comfort. We provide a free load calculation during every estimate.

How can I increase the efficiency of my system?

Keep filters clean, schedule bi-annual professional tune-ups, seal and insulate ductwork, set thermostat setbacks or use a smart thermostat, and keep outdoor units clear of debris. Upgrading to a high-efficiency heat pump can also cut utility costs by 30–50%.

How often should I replace my filters?

Most 1-inch filters need replacement every 1–3 months. Thicker media or HEPA filters can last 6–12 months. Homes with pets, smokers, or allergy sufferers often need more frequent changes.

How often should regular maintenance be done?

Twice a year: once in spring for cooling equipment and once in fall for heating. Preventive maintenance keeps warranties valid, improves efficiency, and can double equipment life.

What are common signs my system needs repair?

Uneven temperatures, rising energy bills, strange noises or odors, frequent cycling, water around the unit, or weak airflow all indicate it’s time for a professional inspection.

What is the typical lifespan of a furnace or heat pump?

With proper maintenance, gas furnaces last 15–20 years, heat pumps 12–15 years, and ductless mini-splits about 20 years. Regular tune-ups and prompt repairs maximize service life.

Should I install a smart thermostat?

Yes — smart thermostats offer remote control, learning schedules, usage reports, and can save 8–15% on heating and cooling costs. Many utility companies provide rebates to offset the cost.

Do you offer financing or rebates?

We partner with financing providers for low-interest monthly payment plans and help customers secure utility and state rebates on qualifying high-efficiency equipment. Ask us during your estimate for current offers.
