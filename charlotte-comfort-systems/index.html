Good to know

Questions we get
before the first visit.

Do your technicians work on commission?

No. Our service technicians are not paid on commission, so you can expect a no-pressure, straightforward sales and service experience.

What brands do you install and service?

We're a Lennox Premier Dealer, a Ruud Pro Partner and a Mitsubishi Diamond Contractor, and we also work with Rheem. Our technicians are prepared to make repairs on your system regardless of the manufacturer.

What areas do you cover?

The Charlotte metro and surrounding areas, with dedicated coverage in Davidson, Huntersville, Mint Hill and Waxhaw. We're licensed and insured across North and South Carolina.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — through Payzer, Synchrony Bank and Service Finance Company, plus Lennox and Ruud manufacturer financing.

Is there a charge for emergency callouts?

Yes. There is an emergency callout charge of $180, payable on arrival. Please confirm current terms with us when you book.

What does the Best Price Guarantee cover?

Find a better price on your next home comfort system and we'll pay you $100. The competing offer must be current, written and signed by a licensed HVAC company operating in NC or SC. Rules and restrictions apply.

What indoor air quality options do you offer?

Air cleaners, air purifiers, humidity control systems, high-efficiency media filters, germicidal lights and climate control monitors.
