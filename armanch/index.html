Ground source

Heat that was already under the building.

A geothermal loop moves heat rather than making it. Below the frost line the ground holds a steady temperature all year — the loop borrows it in winter and gives it back in summer.

  • Air source, water source or ground source Armanch installs all three. Which one suits a building is a calculation, not a preference — and the heat loss and gain work is a service they offer in its own right.
  • Design and permit under the same roof Building mechanical system design, heat loss and gain, and turnkey delivery — so the drawings and the installation don't come from two firms blaming each other.
  • Cold-climate heat pumps A live topic in their own expert-advice writing, alongside the rebates currently available on them in Ontario.
  • Free diagnostic if we can't find it Their standing offer: no service call charge if the fault cannot be diagnosed.

Frequently asked

Before the first visit.

Can I buy a furnace or AC online and install it myself?

Installation needs electrical, gas and refrigerant knowledge plus familiarity with local bylaws and building codes. Getting it wrong creates safety and performance problems — and voids the manufacturer's warranty, which is only valid on a professional installation.

Do I really need maintenance every year?

Preventative maintenance avoids costly repairs later and keeps the system efficient, safe and reliable. Many manufacturer warranties also require a yearly inspection by a certified professional to stay valid.

How do I know I can trust the installer?

Every Armanch installer has completed training and comprehensive testing, has undergone extensive background checks, and is fully insured.

What if I'm not happy with the installation?

Get in touch immediately. Armanch offers a 100% satisfaction guarantee on all installations.

How long does an installation take?

Every project is different, but the aim is to complete a building in one day. Technicians arrive at the agreed time and keep you updated through the day. Armanch recommends the homeowner is present; if that's not possible, someone over 18 needs to be there.

What if you can't work out what's wrong?

Then there's no service call charge. Armanch's standing offer is a free service call if the issue cannot be diagnosed.
