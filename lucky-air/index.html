Commercial kitchen exhaust

A kitchen runs on the air you can't see.

Commercial kitchens are far more demanding than domestic ones. Without proper extraction you get stinging eyes, breathing difficulties and grease settling where it should not.

What business owners should know

A neglected exhaust system is not a comfort problem. It is three separate liabilities:

  • Fire hazard — ducts clogged with grease and grime
  • Insurance claims denied because the system was poorly maintained
  • Health and safety exposure, and the fines that follow

What a proper clean involves

Not a wipe of the filters. The full system:

  • Scraping and cleaning the system top to bottom
  • All ducts and ducting systems
  • Ventilation systems
  • Fans and other components
  • Maintaining and changing filters

What the system actually is

An exhaust hood — or extractor hood — mounted over the stove or cooktop. A mechanical fan evacuates fumes, odours, smoke, heat and steam, then filters the air. When it works, nobody notices it.

Install, repair, maintain

Our team handles all three, and we'll give an honest, reasonable estimate before starting — with a thorough clean-up afterwards. That last part is not a given in this trade.

We can answer

Questions worth asking first.

How often does a commercial kitchen exhaust need cleaning?

It depends on the volume and the type of cooking, so ask for a schedule rather than a number off a website. What matters is that it is regular: clogged grease ducts are a fire hazard, and a poorly maintained system can get an insurance claim denied.

Do you work with builders on new fit-outs?

Yes. We help builders and contractors install HVAC systems and commercial kitchen exhausts — which is easier and cheaper to get right at first fix than to correct afterwards.

Residential as well as commercial?

Both. Lucky Air covers heating, air conditioning and ventilation for residential and commercial needs across Toronto and the GTA, though the commercial and kitchen exhaust side is the specialism.

Do you speak Chinese?

Yes — service is available in English and Chinese.

What happens after the work is finished?

A thorough clean-up. It is stated plainly on their own site and it is worth holding any contractor to.

What else can you advise on?

HVAC system lifespan, energy-efficient systems, air filter maintenance, choosing the right system, and where preventive maintenance actually saves money rather than just costing it.
