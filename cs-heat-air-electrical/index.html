Questions
we get asked

Anything not answered here, ask on the phone. (615) 256-1444

How long have you been in business?

C & S Heat Air & Electrical, Inc. has been providing residential and commercial heating, cooling and electrical services to Nashville and the Middle Tennessee area since 1990.

What areas of Middle Tennessee do you service?

Along with our State license we are also licensed in: Metro Nashville/Davidson County, Brentwood TN, Franklin TN, Williamson County, Murfreesboro TN, Lebanon TN, Hendersonville TN, Gallatin TN, and Sumner County.

What happens if I’m not completely satisfied?

C & S guarantees that you will be completely satisfied or you will not pay a dime.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. We use flat-rate pricing instead of hourly rates. On every service call our skilled, honest and dependable service technicians will review with you all work and costs required to repair your units before the work is performed — professionally, informatively, and without any high pressure sales tactics.

Will you work on a system you didn’t install?

Yes. We repair all brands of heating units and cooling units, and we offer preventative maintenance service plans on them afterwards.

When can you come out, and how long is the window?

We provide 24 hours-a-day, 7 days-a-week, 365 days-a-year service, with 2 hour appointment windows. That is a deliberately proactive approach to service and maintenance, and it is different than the ‘1 hour’ service providers.

Do you actually install geothermal, or just service it?

Both, and we design it. Vertical loops, horizontal loops, pond loops and open loop systems, starting with our IGSHPA certified designers and running through our fully licensed engineering partners. From the smallest to the largest jobs we can find the right fit for your business or home.
