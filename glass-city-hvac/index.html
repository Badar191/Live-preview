Questions we get asked

Straight answers, before
anyone picks up a phone.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service in Toledo?

We offer after-hours emergency scheduling for urgent heating and cooling failures in Toledo and surrounding areas. If your furnace quits in winter or AC fails during a heatwave, call (419) 470-0178 immediately. We prioritize safety concerns and total system breakdowns. We do not run 24/7 emergency visits — after-hours service is by appointment.

How much does furnace repair cost in Toledo?

Furnace repair costs in Toledo typically range from $150–$400 for common issues like igniter replacement, blower motor repairs, flame sensor cleaning, or thermostat problems. More complex repairs like heat exchanger replacement or control board issues cost $400–$800. We provide upfront pricing before starting any work.

What are the best furnace installation options in Northwest Ohio?

High-efficiency gas furnaces (95%+ AFUE) are ideal for Northwest Ohio winters. We install Carrier, Trane, Lennox and Rheem systems with warranties up to 12 years. Typical installations range from $3,500–$7,500 with financing available for qualified buyers.

When should I replace my furnace instead of repairing it?

Consider furnace replacement if your system is 15+ years old, requires frequent repairs, has a cracked heat exchanger, or shows declining efficiency. The $5,000 rule helps: if repair cost times furnace age exceeds $5,000, replacement is more cost-effective.

How often should I schedule furnace maintenance?

Schedule furnace maintenance annually in fall before heating season begins. Regular maintenance prevents breakdowns, improves efficiency by 15–20%, extends equipment life, and maintains warranty coverage.

Do you service all furnace brands?

Yes, we service all major furnace brands including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, York, Bryant, Amana, and more.

Can you improve my home’s indoor air quality?

Yes. We offer comprehensive air quality solutions including high-efficiency air filters, whole-home air purifiers, whole-home dehumidifiers, and ventilation systems.
