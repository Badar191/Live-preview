Questions we get asked

You’ve got
questions.

Every answer below is one we already give in person. If yours isn’t here, the call centre will take it at any hour.

Who answers if I call at two in the morning?

Our call centre is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week with experienced call takers, so you will never have to leave a message on an answering machine. We will also call you 30 minutes in advance of your service technician’s arrival.

Who will I speak to when I call?

One of our call centre members — Nicole, Alma, Ana or Ayla — will pick up, or reach out to you shortly if you use the form.

What happens if I’m not happy with the work?

You receive a written 100% Satisfaction Guarantee stating that if you’re not happy with the quality of our work, we’ll do whatever it takes to make it right — no questions asked.

How do you price a job?

Up front. There are never any hidden charges or last-minute add-on fees, and we charge by the job, not by the hour.

What is a SEER rating?

It is how cooling efficiency is graded. We group it three ways: entry level efficiency, 13–16 SEER; mid-efficiency, 16–18 SEER; high efficiency, 20+ SEER.

My house is over 25 years old. Is that a problem?

It is the work we know best. We specialize in bringing older homes up to code, up-to-date, ready to thrive in a modern, connected world.

Do you do more than heating and air?

Electric, plumbing, heating and air, and remodeling — including kitchen and bathroom remodels, sewer and drain work, and indoor air quality.

Which equipment do you work with?

We are a Lennox® Premier Dealer in San Jose. We also work with Goodman, Daikin, Amana, QuietCool, Attic Breeze, Ember and Krystal Klear Water, plus Generac generators and SPAN electrical panels.

Is financing available?

Yes — residential and commercial financing, through our finance partner Optimus.
