Our hands-on
beginning

Hero Air is a family-owned HVAC company serving Chicagoland, founded by Vladimir Yanev in 2011. It was built with hands-on experience — service, repair and installation in real Chicago homes.

Over the years the company has grown through consistent workmanship, clear communication and doing the job right the first time. The focus is proper diagnosis, correct installation, and getting it right the first time around.

“We are a repair-first business, never pushing unnecessary sales, only what is best for your home.”

Their own words: recognition as one of Entrepreneur’s America’s Favorite Mom & Pop Shops, and a People Love Us company on Yelp for three years running.

Questions
they answer

Answers below are quoted from Hero Air’s own pages. Anything they don’t answer in writing isn’t answered here.

Ask them directly →
What is your diagnostic fee?

Our diagnostic fee is $85. This fee covers the technician’s travel to your home and the first 30 minutes of the assessment.

How do I know what the service will cost?

Every service starts with a diagnostic visit. Once we identify the issue, we provide you with clear options and pricing before any work begins.

Do you repair systems or recommend replacement?

We follow a repair-first approach when it makes sense.

How quickly can you respond to a no-heat emergency?

No-heat calls are prioritised to get you back to comfort as soon as possible.

Do you offer financial assistance for larger jobs?

Yes, we offer financing options for qualifying projects.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance programs?

Yes, customers can enroll in membership plans designed to keep systems running smoothly year-round.

How do you handle old HVAC equipment?

We provide equipment recycling as part of our service.

What brands do you service?

We service a wide range of HVAC brands. This includes Bosch, Ameristar, Armstrong, Ducane, Mitsubishi, and many more. If you don’t see your brand listed, call Hero Air.
