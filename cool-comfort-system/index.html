What we do

Ductwork done properly, cooling that keeps up.

Most “my AC isn't working” calls turn out to be an airflow problem, not a broken unit. We fix the cause — which is usually the ducts.

Custom ductwork

New runs, extensions and full replacements — fabricated and fitted to the actual space, not forced into it.

Duct repair & modification

Leaking joints, crushed runs and renovations that left the airflow all wrong. Rebalanced and sealed.

Air conditioning service

Repair and servicing for central air systems, so the cooling you're paying for actually reaches the rooms.

Ventilation & airflow

Hot rooms, cold rooms, weak vents. We diagnose the airflow properly instead of guessing at the thermostat.

Our specialty

Ductwork is the part everybody skips.

A new air conditioner bolted onto bad ducts is an expensive way to stay uncomfortable. Sizing, sealing and routing are what decide whether the system works — and that's the part we do best.

  • Built for the building Runs measured and fabricated for your space, rather than off-the-shelf lengths bent to fit.
  • Sealed and balanced Leaking joints waste the air you already paid to heat or cool. We seal them and balance the runs.
  • Renovation-friendly Finished basements, additions and converted attics — we reroute around the work you've already done.
  • Homes and businesses The same crew handles residential runs and commercial properties across the east end.

Common questions

Straight answers.

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes are free and there's no obligation attached to them. Call 289-547-9299 or send the form and we'll arrange a time to look at the job.

One room is always hotter than the rest. Can you fix that?

Usually, yes — and usually it's a ductwork problem rather than a faulty air conditioner. Undersized runs, leaking joints or a badly balanced system all cause it. We diagnose the airflow before recommending any new equipment.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We handle ductwork and air conditioning for both residential and commercial properties.

We're renovating. When should we call you?

As early as you can. Rerouting ducts is far cheaper before the walls and ceilings close up, and it means the finished space actually gets proper airflow.

Who will be doing the work?

You'll be dealing with Yousef directly, from the first phone call through to the finished job.
