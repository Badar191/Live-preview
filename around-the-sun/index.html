Do you have maintenance plans?

Yes. Around the Sun Heating & Cooling offers HVAC maintenance plans designed to keep your heating and cooling systems running efficiently year-round. These plans include inspections, cleanings, and professional upkeep to improve system performance and longevity.

What is the Sun Membership maintenance plan?

Members receive 10% off HVAC repairs, 15% off new HVAC units, priority scheduling during peak seasons, and regular system checkups and reminders — year-round comfort and peace of mind.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. Refer a friend and earn a $1,000 Visa Gift Card when they move forward with a new HVAC system installation.

What are your financing options?

$0 down payment options, and no payments for up to 18 months on approved credit.

What is the average cost to replace an AC unit?

On average, replacing an air conditioner can cost anywhere from $4,000 to $10,000, depending on system size and installation complexity. In Gilbert, AZ, most homeowners can expect to pay between $5,000 and $9,000 for a complete air conditioning system installation.

What is the average lifespan of an AC unit in Arizona?

In Gilbert, AZ, the average air conditioner lasts about 12–15 years due to the intense summer heat.

How much does a mini split cost to replace?

Between $3,000 and $10,000, depending on system size and installation complexity.

How many hours does it take to install a furnace?

Four to eight hours. In Gilbert, we aim to complete most installations in a single day.
