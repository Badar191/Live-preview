What we do

Seven things this shop is built to do.

Not a call-centre with a logo. A mechanical contractor that lays out the job, fabricates the metal, installs it and keeps it running — under one roof, for commercial, industrial and marine work.

Sheet metal

Sheet metal fabrication

Steel, stainless steel and aluminum duct systems from 26 to 10 gauge for industrial process systems, HVAC and dust collection — cut on our computerised Plazma machine.

Fiberglass

Fiberglass fabrication

Fabrication and installation of fiberglass duct board and internally insulated sheet metal duct systems.

Install

Installation & service

Installation and service of heating, air conditioning, ventilating and refrigeration equipment.

Layout & design

Layout and design of HVAC and special environmental control systems for computer rooms, classrooms, laboratories and manufacturing areas.

Service

Plant

PM

Preventative Maintenance (PM) contracts

Written with an almost unlimited range of options — the number of inspections per year, hourly or lump-sum rates, and an inspection, assessment and payment schedule built to match the facility.

The metal shop

The duct fits the building, not the other way round.

Fabricating our own sheet metal is a different capability from installing what a supplier ships. Layout and cutting of every system is done with speed and accuracy on our computerised Plazma cutting machine — steel, stainless and aluminum, 26 to 10 gauge.

  • Industrial process, HVAC & dust collection Duct systems fabricated for the job in front of us, not assembled from stock fittings.
  • Fiberglass duct board & insulated duct Fiberglass duct board and internally insulated sheet metal duct systems, fabricated and installed.
  • Environmental control that has to hold Special systems for computer rooms, classrooms, laboratories and manufacturing areas — where the tolerance is the point.
